Levels now unlock things

October 28, 2026, 6:05 PM PST. Built and tested in Studio, not published yet (it would go out as v19).

What changed

  • Reaching certain levels unlocks a reward right away. The message reads, for example, "LEVEL 10 CHECKERED TRAIL UNLOCKED!"
  • QUESTS shows the next three rewards under your level, colored by rarity, so there's always a reason to play one more match.
  • The Garage shows the next level paint as a faded swatch marked with its level (for example "LV20").
  • Players who are already past a level get its reward the next time they join.
  • If you already own the car or horn, you get Gems instead, the same as a duplicate from a crate.

Match counts assume about 170 XP per match (a middle finish with 8 fares). The Double XP pass halves them. Level-only paints and trails can't be bought and never come from crates, so owning one shows how far you've played. Every reward and level is a Config line, so they're easy to move.

Checked in Studio

  • +1000 XP from level 1 reached level 5 and paid 3 rewards at once ("3 LEVEL REWARDS UNLOCKED!"). Gems went from 5 to 55.
  • Another +1000 XP reached level 8: "LEVEL 7 100 GEMS UNLOCKED!" (Gems 155).
  • +2000 XP reached level 11: "LEVEL 10 CHECKERED TRAIL UNLOCKED!"
  • QUESTS showed LV 12 Kazoo horn, LV 15 Horse, LV 20 Silver paint. The Garage showed Bronze owned, a faded LV20 swatch, Checkered under TRAIL and Squeaky under HORN.
  • Bronze paint and the Checkered trail showed on the moving cab.
  • Analytics tagged the Gems "Level:Lv2" and "Level:Lv7". The log showed no errors.

Not checked yet: an older account getting its rewards on join (it runs the same code that paid 3 rewards above), the Gems for a horn or car you already own, and the QUESTS chips on a phone screen.
