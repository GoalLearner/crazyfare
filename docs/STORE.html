1 What players can buy

  • SHOP now has four tabs: PASSES, CARS, BOOSTS, GEMS.
  • Passes: VIP (2x Gems, +25% cash on every delivery, gold name tag), Turbo Tank (double boost tank, fills 50% faster), Armored Seats (passengers take half damage), Double Item (hold 2), Head Start ($10 toward the quota every round), Fare Radar (a $$ beacon over every golden and long fare, through buildings), Pit Stop Plus, 2x XP.
  • Robux cars: Rocket Cab (fastest car: top speed 105, the best free one is 90) and Armored Limo (+40 HP, 30% less damage). Night Cab and Ice Cream Truck can still be bought with Gems too.
  • Boosts while you drive: a strip on the left of the screen (under the map on phones): ITEM, TOMATO x3, NITRO, PATCH UP, +$15. A boost that wouldn't do anything right now is greyed out. If one can't apply anyway (say the round ended mid-purchase), the player gets Gems instead, so nobody pays for nothing.
  • Upgrades go to level 10 now, so Gem packs and the Max Upgrades product (199 R$) are the fast way up. The Starter Pack also raises every upgrade to level 3.
