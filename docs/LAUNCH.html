CrazyFare launch checklist

October 27, 2026. Live now: version 5 (phases A to E). Phase F is built and tested in Studio, not published yet.

1 Friends test (now, on version 5)

  • Press ARCADE between matches. It should send you to a server of your own (the first time this runs live). RACE brings you back.
  • Do a second Arcade run that beats your first: the plate should say NEW BEST! and the PROGRESS panel's BEST ARCADE RUN board should show it.
  • Listen: four music tracks rotate, and short stings play on a combo of 5, a Golden Fare, a new best and a win. Tell me any that sound bad.

! Server size: 6 players do this before anyone else plays

The city has 6 start pads and the race is built for 6, so each server should let in 6 players. Roblox opens more servers by itself as more people play, so this does not limit how many play in total.

  1. Creator Hub, then Creations, then CrazyFare, then Configure, then Places, then the CrazyFare place.
  2. Set Max Players to 6 and save.

If more ever get in, the game parks the extra cars beside the grid instead of on top of each other, but 6 is the right size.

4 Let under-16 players in your account, your call

New games stay 16+ until they pass Roblox's review. The steps are yours (I never spend money or enter your details):

  1. Verify your ID and turn on 2-step verification on fitxvert.
  2. Reach 250 plays from engaged players within 60 days: friends first, maybe a small ad later.
  3. Pay the refundable 1,000 Robux fee, or have Roblox Premium for 2 months.

The maturity questionnaire is already answered: mild cartoon violence, repeated, no blood.
