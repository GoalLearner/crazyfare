Server and lobby flow, version 1

October 27, 2026, 9:00 PM PST. Built while you were at the gym. Tested in Studio, committed, not published. Live is still v14.

  2. Set Max Players to 10 (it was 6) and save. 6 people race; the other 4 walk the city and hail cabs until a seat opens. The game never puts more than 6 on the grid.

1 What a player sees now

  • Taxi Depot. Join between matches and you drive your own car in a yard beside the City: a cone ring to drift around, two ramps, a cone slalom, and a board with this server's longest jump and best drift. Up top: NEXT MATCH IN 0:08 and the 6 seats filling with people, then bots. Tap STAY IN DEPOT to sit a match out, JOIN THE MATCH to come back.
  • Joining mid-round. A free seat: you drive right away, $10 behind last place, and a bot leaves to make room. Banner: YOU'RE IN! CATCH UP!
  • Server full (6 driving). You get your Roblox character on a sidewalk. Cars pass through you (you dive out of the way when one is about to hit you). Banner: RIDE ALONG UNTIL A SEAT OPENS.
  • HAIL CAB. Near a curb during a round. You become a gold fare with a TAXI! sign that every driver (and bot) sees. The ride pays 1.5x. You sit in the cab, see the driver's trip (fare card, arrow, countdown, stunts) and get tossed out if the passenger bails. On arrival you rate the driver 1 to 5 stars (the tip goes from 0.8x to 1.3x) and earn 3 Gems.
  • Joining during the Pit Stop. You walk until the next round, then take your seat: YOU DRIVE NEXT ROUND.
  • Between matches. No more REMATCH vote: after a 12 s podium the next match starts by itself (under 20 s in all). People who waited get seats first, bots fill the rest. INVITE is on the podium and in the depot; a friend who follows you in gets the next free seat.
  • New COLLECTION button (cars owned, best license, lifetime stats). PROGRESS is now called QUESTS.

2 What I saw working in Studio tested

  • 1 player: the depot, the board recording a jump and a drift, WAITING FOR DRIVERS after STAY, the countdown with me plus 3 bots after JOIN, the hand-off to the grid, a full match, and the next match starting by itself on the Beach.
  • 2 players with 1 seat: the second player on foot by a pickup, HAIL CAB with the ring and TAXI! sign (visible to the driver), the pickup ($27 for a long trip = $18 x 1.5), the rider's chase camera with the trip's fare card and no money, a timeout tossing the rider out, a delivery, the 5-star prompt and +3 Gems, and the next match giving the seat to the one who had waited.
  • Late joins: a player added mid-round drove at once at $70.11 (last place $80.11 minus $10). With 6 people and 3 seats, 3 drove and 3 walked. No script errors.
  • Fixed during the tests: the dodge triggered for every car passing in the lane; the minimap showed in the depot; a newcomer could take a seat promised to someone waiting.
