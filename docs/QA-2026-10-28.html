Bug hunt: 6 bots, today's features, smarter bots

October 28, 2026, evening PT. 30 Studio matches of bots racing on their own (6 bots, and 4 like a live grid), me driving every new feature, and a separate code review of today's work. Everything below is committed but not published (it would be v20).

Bugs found and fixed

  • STAY IN DEPOT covered the 5th and 6th rows on the podium (you spotted it). The podium now grows with the number of drivers, so the buttons and countdown always sit below the last row. fixed, seen in Studio
  • Weirdo cards never showed up in a full match. With 6 drivers every curb already has a passenger, so the bonus passenger had nowhere to stand. It now takes a regular passenger's place. fixed
  • Bots froze behind a stopped car. Two bots nose to nose, or a bot behind a parked player, waited forever (one round, two bots never moved). They now swing around after 2 seconds. fixed, 0 stuck bots since
  • Bots shoved and flipped parked players. My parked cab was pushed about 85 studs and flipped. Bots now give player cars a wide berth. fixed, seen in Studio
  • Trails didn't show in the Taxi Depot. The depot is where you'd try a new trail. It now shows there too. fixed, seen in Studio
  • Your streak could reset when you were still playing at midnight UTC. The streak only advanced when you joined, so a session that ran past 00:00 UTC (5 PM PT, prime time) didn't count the new day. Your next visit could then reset you to DAY 1. Players still in the game now claim the new day automatically. fixed
  • Phantom serial numbers could repeat. If Roblox's data service hiccuped, a new owner could get a duplicate "#1" saved for good. Two grants at once could also each take a number. Now a failed fetch tries again on the owner's next join. fixed
  • Map vote stayed open after the podium fell back to the depot, so old votes picked a later match's map. fixed
  • Analytics: older accounts would have replayed the new-player funnel once and inflated launch numbers. They now skip it. fixed

Bots now drive like players

  • They cut straight across corners, open lots, parks and the plaza whenever the way is clear. They never go through buildings, walls, solid props or water, and they don't take sand "shortcuts" (sand slows them like everyone).
  • Shorter breathers between fares: 1 to 2.5 s instead of 3 to 6 s.
  • They hurry with a passenger aboard (speed 32.5, against 26 when empty), the way you race the meter. That keeps timeouts low without making them quicker overall.
  • Smarter routing had made bots too fast: at the old speed of 36, a bot reached the first $75 in about 1:50, the same pace that beat you on 10/27. Now the best bot takes about 2:07.

Today's features, checked

  • All 9 cars spawn and drive (in the depot and in races): Yellow Cab, Go-Kart, Shopping Cart, Horse, Night Cab, Ice Cream Truck, Rocket Cab, Armored Limo, the Phantom.
  • The Phantom: ghost-horse model, shield bubble, "A PHANTOM HAS ENTERED THE MATCH", owners board serial #1, its badge (prints in Studio), Garage line "ONLY 1 OWNS THE PHANTOM · YOURS IS #1".
  • Drops and cosmetics: end-of-match crates (Bronze for last place), card, trail and horn drops; equipping trails and horns; Chrome paint on the Go-Kart; Checkered, Hot Sauce and Rainbow trails on the car.
  • Level rewards, the streak calendar (days 1, 4, 7, duplicate day 7), the daily shop, lifetime fares on name tags, the map vote card, City → Beach rotation.
  • On an iPhone 7 screen (Studio's simulator): the calendar card, the SHOP DAILY tab and the QUESTS reward chips all fit.
  • Passenger perks (Phantom +60 HP, 60% less damage, banana and oil immunity, triple hunter bonus, shield vs tomato): verified by reading the code end to end, not live. Picking up passengers by hand in Studio took too long.

Health

  • 30 matches, 101 rounds, 2,170 bot pickups: 0 script errors on the server and the client, across City and Beach, every weather, Rush Hour and money-truck events.
  • No leaks found: over the last 14-match run, server memory leveled off around 1.54 GB, and the workspace returns to the same instance count each time the map comes back around.
  • The worst server freeze in the server-only runs was about 0.36 s, at boot and between matches. One 1.8 s freeze showed up once at a City to Beach switch early in the night; worth watching on live servers.
  • Since the fixes, no bot got stuck in the last 50+ rounds. A bot can still bump a parked car once in a while, but none were shoved far or flipped.

Still worth a look
