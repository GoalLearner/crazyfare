Title screen

October 27, 2026, 11:25 PM PST. Built, tested in Studio, committed, not published. Live is still v14.

1 What a player sees

  • Loading screen (ours, not Roblox's grey one): the CRAZYFARE logo, a spinning tire, LOADING THE CITY...
  • Title screen on every join: a drone circles high over the live city, so the real race, bots and traffic play underneath, with a warm golden tint.
  • The logo in chunky gold letters with a taxi checker stripe, and a yellow joke line pulsing off its corner (a different one of 20 each time: TIP YOUR DRIVER!, THE METER IS RUNNING!, LEFT AT THE PIZZA!...).
  • Buttons: PLAY, GARAGE, OPTIONS, CREDITS. One line under them says what's going on right now: ROUND 2 · 4 DRIVING, or NEXT MATCH IN 0:08.
  • PLAY swoops the camera down to your car in the depot (or your seat, or the sidewalk), and the HUD comes back.
  • OPTIONS: music and sound volume, camera shake on/off, speed effects on/off. Saved to the account. Also in the side buttons between matches on computers.
  • CREDITS: "A GAME BY fitxvert", the city art (Kenney), music (APM) and sound effects (Pro Sound Effects).
  • Someone sitting on the title never takes a seat, so the grid isn't held up by people reading the menu.

2 What I saw working in Studio tested

  • The title over the city with no HUD or minimap; OPTIONS turned the music off at once; CREDITS showed your Roblox name; GARAGE opened over the title.
  • PLAY swooped into the depot car and the next-match countdown started with 3 bots.
  • Four test players: two stayed on the title while a match ran ("ROUND 1 · 4 DRIVING" = one person and 3 bots), and one who pressed PLAY mid-round was seated at once.
  • On an iPhone 7 (a small phone) the buttons fit and the logo sits clear of Roblox's buttons.
  • Fixed while testing: the drone flew past the radio mast (a red wall across the screen), every player got the same joke line, the logo slid under Roblox's buttons on a phone, and the OPTIONS side button covered REVERSE on a phone.

3 Your Studio test

  1. Test, then Start Test Session, then Test (or Server and Clients for 2+). You land on the title.
  2. Try OPTIONS (music down to nothing), CREDITS and GARAGE, then PLAY.
  3. To skip the title while testing other things: in the command bar, workspace:SetAttribute('SkipTitle', true) before you start (clear it with nil).
