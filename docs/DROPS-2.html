2 The weirdo bonus fare

  • A card you didn't have sends that weirdo to a curb 6 seconds into your next round. They're in costume (shirt and hat in the card's colors, the card's icon on their badge, their name above it).
  • They're a long fare that pays double. The payout line says WEIRDO BONUS.
  • For the first 20 seconds they're yours alone. Other players can't see the badge or the minimap dot, Fare Radar skips them, and nobody else (bots included) can pick them up. After that, it's anyone's fare.
  • You get a banner: "YOUR GHOST IS WAITING!"

5 Something I found that was already broken

Paint doesn't change a player's Yellow Cab. The cab model players drive was imported without paintable parts, so any paint, bought for 50 Gems or won from a crate, does nothing on the default car. Bots' cabs and the other cars do take paint. This is how the live game works today too, so players can pay Gems for paint that doesn't show.

I can fix it so paint (and the new Chrome, Gold and other paints) shows on the Yellow Cab. It changes how the cab model is prepared in Studio, so I'd check the look with you before publishing.
