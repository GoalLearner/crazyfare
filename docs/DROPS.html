Post-match drops, part 1 of 3

October 28, 2026, 11:45 AM PST. Built, tested in Studio, committed, not published. Live is still v14. Next: part 2 (trails, horns, Weirdo cards), then part 3 (the Phantom car).

1 What players get

  • A free crate after every match, on the podium. 1st place gets a Gold crate, 2nd and 3rd Silver, everyone else Bronze. Better crates have better odds.
  • The crate drops in beside the podium, shakes, bursts in its rarity's color and shows the prize. Already own it? It says ALREADY YOURS +N GEMS.
  • Prizes so far: Gems, paints (including four you can only get from drops: Sunset, Lime, Bubblegum, Teal), "start your next match holding a Tomato" style tokens, the Gem cars at Epic, Rocket Cab and Armored Limo at Legendary, 2,500 Gems or the Phantom at Mythic.
  • Pity: an Epic or better is guaranteed by the 30th drop since your last one.
  • The i button shows every crate's odds and the Phantom's chance (1 in 23,256 from Bronze, 7,752 from Silver, 4,651 from Gold; about 1 in 10,000 overall).
  • A Mythic pull puts a gold banner ("FITXVERT PULLED THE PHANTOM!"), a flash and a chime on every screen in the server.
  • Free and earned only: nothing can buy, reroll or improve a crate, VIP doesn't double drop Gems, and bots get none.

2 What I saw working in Studio tested

  • Every rarity's reveal, the Phantom banner, the odds card, duplicates turning into Gems.
  • Pity: 5 times out of 5, a player one drop away from the guarantee got an Epic.
  • The odds are right: 100,000 simulated Gold crates gave 0.503% Mythic and 21 Phantoms (expected 0.5% and about 21).
  • Real match ends: 1st place opened a Gold crate, 2nd place a Silver one, beside the podium. One real Gold crate even rolled the Phantom (genuine luck).
  • On a small phone the crate fits left of the podium (it brushes the money counter on the smallest screens).
  • Fixed: the Starter Pack card covered the podium; it now shows only in the depot.

3 Your Studio test

  1. Test, PLAY, then in the left debug panel scroll to Drop: Common ... Drop: Mythic, Phantom pull, 30 drops, Pity 29 then 1 drop.
  2. Finish a match (the debug End round button, then READY at the Pit Stop) to see the real crate on the podium.
  3. Press the i on the crate card for the odds.
