CrazyFare, Phase 1 is built

Project at C:\dev\crazyfare. Nothing has run in Studio yet, so the first play test is yours. Steps below, then the five knobs for car feel.

1. One-time setup (about 3 minutes)

Open a terminal (PowerShell is fine) and run these from the project folder. rokit, rojo, wally, selene and luau-lsp are already installed on this PC; the packages are already pulled. The only new thing is the Studio plugin.

cd C:\dev\crazyfare
rojo plugin install

If a terminal says rojo is not recognised, open a new terminal first (rokit added itself to PATH when it was installed).

2. Run a 2-player test

  1. In the terminal, from C:\dev\crazyfare: 
    rojo serve
    Leave it running. It says Listening on 127.0.0.1:34872 (port may differ).
  2. Open Roblox Studio → New → Baseplate. Any empty place works; the city is generated from code.
  3. Studio top bar → Plugins tab → Rojo → Connect. The Rojo panel turns green and ReplicatedStorage/Shared, ServerScriptService/Server and StarterPlayer/StarterPlayerScripts/Client appear in the Explorer.
  4. Test tab → Clients and Servers → set Players to 2 → Start. Three windows open: one server, two players.
  5. In each player window you should see: the blocked-out city, your yellow cab on the start grid, READ THE BOARD, then 3 2 1 GO. Drive with WASD.
What to try in order
  1. Stop next to a passenger (the figures with a coloured $ over their head). Stopping inside 12 studs claims them. The other player stopping at the same one sees TAKEN.
  2. Follow the arrow under the timer to the tall coloured beacon. Stop inside it. The payout punches on screen with the tip and combo.
  3. Drive through a glowing cyan box to get an item. Q uses it. Tomato homes on the nearest loaded rival and splats their windshield; H honks the wipers faster. Banana drops behind you.
  4. Hold Space in a corner to drift: the combo meter fills, so does the boost tank. Shift spends the tank.
  5. Get a rival's passenger to 0 HP (tomato + walls) and watch them bail and walk to the nearest curb.
  6. First to $30 ends the round, or the 4-minute cap does. Podium, then a new round starts on its own.
  7. The DEBUG panel on the left (Studio only) gives items, damages or heals your passenger, adds cash and force-ends the round.

Flipped? R resets the car (it also auto-resets after 2.5 s upside down). Map looks wrong? Stop the test and in the command bar run require(game.ServerScriptService.Server.Studio.DevBlockout).Generate() to see errors directly.

3. The first 5 knobs for car feel

All in src\shared\Config.luau. Rojo pushes edits live; restart the test to apply them. Change one at a time and drive a lap.

KnobNowWhat it doesTry
Vehicles.Stats.YellowCab.TopSpeed90Max studs/s on the flat. The map is 600 studs across; a red fare is a 10-second straight at this speed.80 feels heavier, 100 feels loose
Vehicles.Stats.YellowCab.Accel45Launch punch. Time to top speed is roughly TopSpeed ÷ Accel × 1.5 because the push fades as you approach the cap.35 for weight, 60 for arcade
Vehicles.Stats.YellowCab.Handling6Lateral grip rate. Higher snaps the car to its heading; lower lets it slide on every turn.4 = slippery, 9 = on rails
Vehicles.Stats.YellowCab.SteerMax32°Wheel angle at standstill. Chassis.SpeedSteerFalloff (0.55) removes that much of it at top speed.28 if it spins out, 38 if corners feel wide
Vehicles.Stats.YellowCab.DriftGrip0.25Share of Handling the rear tyres keep while Space is held. Lower = longer, wilder drifts.0.15 for Mario Kart, 0.4 for a tidy slide

Next layer after those: Chassis.SuspensionStiffness (2.5, bouncier lower), Chassis.DriftSteerBonus (1.3), Camera.Distance / Height, then the fares: Fares.Tiers.*.Base and Tips.MaxFrac.

4. Decisions I made so I could build without waiting

Nothing truly blocked Phase 1. These are the defaults; each is one line in Config or PLAN.md to flip.

  • No avatars. Players have no character; the taxi is the player. Cleaner physics, no jumping out of seats. A seated avatar can come back later for cosmetics.
  • Controls: WS throttle/brake, AD steer, Space drift, Shift boost tank, Q item, H honk, R reset. Basic gamepad mapped.
  • Boost tank fills from drifting and near-misses; Shift spends it. Lemon Nitro is a separate instant burst.
  • Fare timer hits zero = the passenger bails (Crazy Taxi rules). Flag Fares.TimeoutBails switches it to "pays base, no tip".
  • Tomato is the only item that costs your own passenger 15 HP when fired loaded. Banana is a trap, not an attack.
  • Can't pick up a box while holding an item. Boxes respawn after 10 s.
  • Solo testing works: rounds auto-start with 1 player. Set Round.MinPlayers = 2 before publishing.
  • Phase 1 loops single rounds (podium, then a new round). The 3-round match and Pit Stop are Phase 3, as in your plan.

The full list of 15 is in docs\PLAN.md under "Decisions made instead of asking".

5. Two things only you can answer not blocking
