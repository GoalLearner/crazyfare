CrazyFare: the parts only you can do

All six phases are built and the game is published (private). These are the steps that need your Roblox account.

1. Publish the place · done 10/26

Published as CrazyFare, private, owned by you.

  • Game page: roblox.com/games/75634679424787
  • Creator Hub: experience 10773213053

Changes I make reach the live game only when it's published again: Studio, File → Publish to Roblox (Alt+P). Or ask me.

Live servers save Gems and garage on their own. Studio tests reset them unless you turn on File → Experience Settings → Security → Enable Studio Access to API Services. Before going public, set max players to 6 in Creator Hub → Configure → Places.

2. Get three friends into a test

  1. Be Roblox friends with them.
  2. Open the Creator Hub link above → Publish settings → Manage Audience → Manage access (top right) → Public tab → turn on Friends → Save Changes. Roblox may first ask for an age check and one question from the maturity questionnaire; your account must be at least 2 days old.
  3. Send them the game page link. They press Play.
  4. To land in the same server: you join first, then they join you from your profile's Join button. With only friends allowed, pressing Play close together usually works too.

A match starts once two of you are in. Anyone who joins later drops into the round in progress; after the match, everyone presses Rematch and the next one starts together.

What the test is for: is it fun with 4? How do the cars feel? Are fares and tips worth chasing? Tell me what you notice.

If Roblox asks the maturity questionnaire (it can before Friends or Public), answer honestly:

  • Violence: yes, mild. Tomatoes splat windshields; pedestrians you clip hop, spin and yell. Nobody falls, gets hurt or dies.
  • How often: repeated (it can happen many times a round).
  • Blood: none.

That keeps CrazyFare in the Mild band. A pedestrian that collapses or ragdolls would push it to Moderate, so tell me before changing how a hit looks.
